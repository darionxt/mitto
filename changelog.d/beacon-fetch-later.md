- A Page, whose API stays private, sends `navigator.sendBeacon()`'s POST as Chrome does, as a keepalive request of the Kind `beacon` with the body that Blink's beacons write, where it was a Known Gap that sent nothing and returned `false`: in mode `no-cors`, or `cors` for a `Blob` of a type that is not CORS-safelisted, within the keepalive quota of the document, and it returns `false` past that quota, as Chrome's does ([runtime.md](docs/runtime.md#fetch)).
- A Page, whose API stays private, has `fetchLater()` as Chrome does, where it was a Known Gap whose result never went: its Session holds the keepalive fetch and sends it once `activateAfter` has passed, when its document goes, when the back/forward cache takes its document or when its Page ends, drops it when its signal aborts, and its `FetchLaterResult` says `activated` then. It throws Chrome's errors, and a `QuotaExceededError` past the quota of its deferred-fetch control document, as Chrome works it out for frames: 64 KiB for each origin, of 512 KiB in a top-level document and of 8 KiB in a frame of another origin. The Session holds up to 1,920 KiB of them for a Page, three times what those quotas let a page queue, and drops one past that without a word, which only a long method, which no quota counts, can take it to ([runtime.md](docs/runtime.md#fetch)).
