- A Page, whose API stays private, loads an `iframe` whose `credentialless` attribute is set as a credentialless frame, as Chrome 157 does, where the attribute did nothing: `window.credentialless` is true in it and in each frame under it, `document.hasStorageAccess()` gives false, and its requests, cookies and storage are partitioned by a nonce of the top-level document, so that a credentialless frame neither sends nor sees the cookies and storage of the page or of the Session, and shares its own with the page's other credentialless frames of its origin until the top-level document goes. Its cookies never reach the Session's cookies, and its storage stays in memory. An embedder policy of `require-corp` or `credentialless` lets such a frame load whatever its response's policy says ([runtime.md](docs/runtime.md#cross-origin-isolation)).
