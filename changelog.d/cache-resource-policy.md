- A Page, whose API stays private, holds an opaque response that its Cache API matches to the response's `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy` under an embedder policy of `require-corp` or `credentialless`, as Chrome does, where it gave every one: `Cache.match()`, `Cache.matchAll()` and `CacheStorage.match()` reject with a `TypeError` when one of the responses that they would give is blocked, and `keys()` gives requests as before. Under a report-only embedder policy alone, a response whose own `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy` does not let the context have it is blocked so too, as the check runs for such a context, though its missing policy is not ([runtime.md](docs/runtime.md#cross-origin-isolation)).
